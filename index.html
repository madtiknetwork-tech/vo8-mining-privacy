Privacy Policy for VO8 Mining Simulator

Effective Date: October 18, 2026

VO8 Mining Simulator ("we", "our", or "us") provides the VO8 Mining Simulator mobile app as an ad-supported and free simulated mining service. This page informs users regarding our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data for anyone deciding to use our Service.

1. Information Collection and Storage

To provide core application features and synchronize your simulated balances across sessions, we collect and process the following information:

  • Account Authentication: When registering or logging in, we collect your email address and profile name via Firebase Authentication to verify your account identity.
  • Simulated Mining State & Balance: Simulated coin balances, active cycle timestamps, and ad milestone counters are stored locally on your device (via SharedPreferences) and synced securely to Cloud Firestore.
  • Device & Network Identifiers: Anonymous device state, operating system version, and network availability checks are performed to prevent automated account abuse and ensure offline/online state handling.

Note: This application is strictly a simulation. We do not process financial transactions, bank cards, or paid investment deposits.

2. Third-Party Services and SDKs

Our app integrates third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device or serve advertisements:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore)
  • Google AdMob (Advertisements)

3. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your profile data as long as your account remains active. Users may request full account deletion and removal of their associated Firestore data at any time by emailing us at: madtiknetwork@gmail.com.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so that we can delete such records.
